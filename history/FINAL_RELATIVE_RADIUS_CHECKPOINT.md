## How the energy bound is proved

1. **Equal x-values give an `SL(2, ℤ)` relation.**
   - Suppose `x(τ₁) = x(τ₂)`, with `P(x) ≠ 0`.
   - The identities `E₄P(x) = A²N(x)` and `E₆P(x)² = A³T(x)` then give `E₄(τ₁) = μ⁴E₄(τ₂)` and `E₆(τ₁) = μ⁶E₆(τ₂)`.
   - Since `G_k(ℤτ+ℤ) = 2ζ(k)E_k(τ)`, the lattices `ℤτ₁ + ℤ` and `μ⁻¹(ℤτ₂ + ℤ)` have the same `g₂, g₃`.
   - The uniqueness of `℘` then makes them equal, which yields `τ₁ = γτ₂` with `γ ∈ SL(2, ℤ)`.
2. **Generic points give `Γ₀(7)`.** The relation `x(−1/z) = 1/(49x(z/7))` excludes the seven non-identity cosets almost everywhere on each horocycle.
3. **Jensen on the q-disc.** For `q ↦ x(q) − x(q′)`, every zero is `e^{2πiγτ′}` with `γ ∈ Γ₀(7)`, and it is simple. Its contribution is `2π(y/|cτ′+d|² − t)₊`.
4. **Averaging.**
   - Average over the circle using the coprime-residue count.
   - Evaluate the real integral in arccosine form.
   - The result is `∫ U_j dμ_i ≤ E(y_i, y_j)`, which gives `I ≤ Σ w_i w_j E(y_i, y_j)`.
   - The equality in (48), the lower bound, is not formalized. It is not needed.
